# Macro Spellbook // Test Plan

Macro Spellbook adds a tab to your spellbook that turns your class's spells into one-click macros for your bars. This time we most want you to try it on every game version, along with the new right-click macro options and the options under /msb.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, MoP Classic and Retail.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Right-click a macro on the page and try each group: Combat Check, Spell Rank, Targeting, Before Casting, Add Attack, Casting Rules, Error Messages and Use Items First.
2. Set a buff's Spell Rank to Dynamic Rank, then target or mouse over a low-level friend; it should pick a rank they can take.
3. Tick Right-Click For on a buff like Power Word: Fortitude or Arcane Intellect, then right-click it on your bar to cast the group buff.
4. Duplicate a macro from its right-click menu, give the copy different options, then use Remove This Copy.
5. Pick Use These Options For All from a macro's right-click menu; Macro Defaults should open showing them.
6. Type /msb, add a macro under Custom Macros, and check it shows on the page under Custom, or tells you why not.
7. Use Set Key in the options to bind Toggle Macro Spellbook, then use the key to open and close the page.
8. In the page's settings menu, use Mark All Spells As Placed, then Clear Placed Marks; the glow should go away and come back.
9. If you play in a language other than English, check the page and its menus read in your language.

## Everything Else

10. Open your spellbook, click the Macro Spellbook tab, and click or drag a macro onto your bars; it should stop glowing.
11. Learn a new spell at your trainer and check its macro appears on the page.
12. On a class with stances, forms or a pet, try Switch To, Leave Form Before Casting and Start Pet Attack.
13. Fight with your macros, **especially on WoW Forever**, and check Only In Combat, Cast Once Per Combat and Stop Macro If Channeling work.
14. Change a macro's options during a fight and check the macro on your bar updates once the fight ends.
15. Open the options with /msb and with Macro Defaults in the page's settings menu, then type /msb in combat; it should refuse with a chat message.
