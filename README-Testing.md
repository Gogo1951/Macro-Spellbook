# Macro Spellbook // Test Plan

Macro Spellbook adds a tab to your spellbook that turns your class's spells into one-click macros for your bars. This time we most want you to try the new right-click macro options and the game versions it now runs on.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, MoP Classic and Retail.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Right-click a macro on the page and try the new groups: Combat Check, Spell Rank, Targeting, Casting Rules and Error Messages.
2. Set a heal or buff to Dynamic Rank and cast it on a low-level friend; it should use a lower rank.
3. Right-click a buff like Power Word: Fortitude or Arcane Intellect, tick Right-Click For its group buff, then right-click the macro on your bar to cast the group buff.
4. Type /msb, add a macro under Custom Macros, and check it shows on the page under Custom.
5. Set a key under Key Bindings in the options and use it to open and close Macro Spellbook.
6. In the page's settings menu, use Mark All Spells As Placed, then Clear Placed Marks; the glow should go away and come back.
7. If you play in a language other than English, check the page and its menus read in your language.

## Everything Else

8. Open your spellbook, click the Macro Spellbook tab, and click or drag a macro onto your bars; it should stop glowing.
9. Learn a new spell at your trainer and check its macro appears on the page.
10. On a class with stances, forms or a pet, try Switch To, Leave Form Before Casting and Enable Pet Attack.
11. Fight with your macros, **especially on WoW Forever**, and check options like Only In Combat and Cast Once Per Combat work.
12. Change a macro's options during a fight and check the macro on your bar updates once the fight ends.
13. Open the options with /msb and with Open Options in the page's settings menu.
14. Type /msb in combat; it should refuse with a chat message.
