# Macro Spellbook

Mouseover macros, cursor casting and self-cast macros for every class spell, built in one click from a new spellbook tab with no macro syntax. Right-click to add focus, spell rank, downrank, stopcasting, auto attack, pet attack or trinket use.

**TL;DR:** Never write a mouseover macro again. Every spell you know is already a macro, so pick your options and drag it onto your bars.

## Setup

1. Install the add-on, ideally using [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/macro-spellbook).
2. Type `/msb` to open the Options Interface.
3. Open your spellbook and click the Macro Spellbook tab.
4. Right-click any macro to pick its options, like Cast On Mouseover or Cast At Cursor.
5. Click or drag the macro onto your action bars.
6. _"Whoa. I know mouseover macros."_

## Features

🖱️ **Mouseover & Cursor Casting** // Heal whoever you hover, hit the mob under your mouse, and drop Blizzard or Hurricane right at your cursor. No target swapping.

⚙️ **Options With a Right-Click** // Add stopcasting, auto attack, pet attack, focus or your trinkets with a tick. The macro rewrites itself, and warns you before it gets too long.

📈 **Smart Spell Ranks** // Automatically update your abilities to use max rank after you train, or dynamically adjust your buffs pick the highest rank your friendly target can receive.

📚 **Your Whole Kit in One Place** // Macros glow until they're on your bars, and the page also holds your on-use gear, your own custom macros, and spells you haven't trained yet.

🦺 **Safety First** // Only ever edits the macros it made. Your own macros stay untouched, even ones that share a spell's name.

## Tips & Tricks

### Group Buffs on Right-Click

Turn on a buff's group version and a right-click casts Arcane Brilliance, Prayer of Fortitude, Gift of the Wild or a Greater Blessing, while a left-click still casts the single target one. Mages get Portals on their Teleports the same way.

### Key Binding

Set a key for Toggle Macro Spellbook in the game's Key Bindings to jump straight to the page with one key press.

### Game Settings It Touches

Mute Error Sounds turns off your Sound Effects and Error Speech settings for a split second while that macro runs, so a spell that isn't ready doesn't whoosh at you, then puts them back exactly as they were.
